# ADR-072: Offline representation and indexed Topic matching rethink

Status: frozen representation experiments measured; indexed diagonal v2 integrated
owner-locally; broad BODY and dual-floor activation rejected; teacher transfer
and separately frozen practical follow-up measured, neither activated.
Date: 2026-10-10.

## Direction and authorization

The owner requests GPT-6.1 Sol to revisit multilingual topic identity,
independently and with high reasoning, including useful local downloads.
This continues the standing owner-local research and fitted-weight use
approval. It does not authorize publisher article uploads to an LLM,
provider spending, training-data/model publication, deployment or store
submission. Article text stays in private local RAM during these experiments;
official frozen model assets may be downloaded into a named OS TEMP folder.
No remote model code is executed. Only checked scripts and aggregate results
may enter Git. The canonical database and Insight write authority are intact.

## Why revisit

The existing .04 competitor lead can reject correct translation pairs in
dense neighborhoods. ADR-071's selectable alternate read view removes that
lead but its .90/.94 cosine gates still reflect generic semantics, not a
calibrated same-event probability. Its diagonal adapter has mixed catalog
results, and a dense all-pair implementation exhausts work at roughly 1,414
eligible pages. A cluster can have unlimited members in principle yet the
catalog can still hit a global implementation limit. Both representation
and execution need separate evidence.

## Parallel, bounded experiments

1. **Vector-only metric:** train-only body-E5 centering and regularized
   global/within-event covariance whitening. Fit on the same 475 approved
   reports; keep the original 384-coordinate vectors immutable. Compare
   raw E5, current diagonal and five fixed variants under identical existing
   graph/calibration code. Select on reused development, freeze, then test
   once on new whole-event reports. This can operate on existing vectors
   without revisiting pages, but the research support graph is not itself
   a production sparse-pair admission policy.
2. **Compact encoder:** compare a pinned multilingual paraphrase MiniLM q8
   encoder with E5, including a 128-token E5 control. The replacement-sized
   model is roughly 118 MB, not a production second model. Test reused
   synthetic reports and separately calibrated real body prefixes. Strongest-
   edge complete-link has no fixed neighbor/member count. A new coordinate
   space would require versioned recapture, never reinterpret old E5 vectors.
3. **Joint title evidence:** test an official multilingual cross-encoder
   with a small fitted symmetric relation head. Separate relevance from
   event identity; compare vector-only, relevance, joint representation and
   combined evidence. Its extra model, latency and retained-title limitations
   must be justified; download permission is not default-promotion evidence.
4. **Indexed planner:** exact geometric candidate retrieval, lazy score
   caching and merge ordering, without an all-pair matrix or a numeric page/
   neighbor/member ceiling. Exact duplicate vectors count as one evidence
   unit while preserving every Source ID. Work exhaustion returns no partial
   partition. Check small-case parity, weak bridges, duplicates and large
   fictional clouds before considering the alternate read path.

## Evidence boundaries

Existing synthetic and 150-report real validation slices are development,
not fresh validation. The new real comparisons share the same whole-event
cohort, excluding the original, preliminary, title-fresh and body-fresh
selections and overlapping title/lead keys. Freeze implementation hashes,
model selection and cutoffs before inference on that slice; no test-led
rescue or retuning. A fresh slice of GlobeSumm is still the same corpus,
not independently verified publisher/viewpoint gold. Report correct grouped
pairs, pure-page reach, wrong grouped pairs and mixed-page exposure, not
just nearest-neighbor accuracy or a complete-event quota.

Research safety criteria are comparative and exploratory. Sparse calibration
negatives, synthetic writing templates, input mismatch with Chrome capture,
and false-bridge amplification must remain visible. A useful relative gain
need not find every match. An unchanged baseline can remain selectable even
when a better candidate is tried owner-locally. Security-sensitive integration
requires an independent Trust review; canonical rerouting, expanded retained
data, distribution and later platform gates remain separate.

## Measured choices, not default promotion

BODY `within-shrink-50` gave 374 correct /0 wrong grouped pairs and 155 pure
pages on 296 fresh reports under the common double-support research graph.
The separately frozen radius/complete-link comparison yielded 1,042 /2 and 259
pure pages, versus raw 78 /4 and 116. Those policies must not be conflated.
The proposed indexed BODY policy then failed reused adjacent-story/viewpoint
stress: C108 218 correct /240 wrong, 69 mixed pages; v6 120 /125, 35 mixed.
Therefore normal startup and dashboard do **not** activate BODY from an installed
file. The explicit research loader requires literal `useBodyMetric: true`.
The reviewed 475-train-report artifact is ignored and contains weights only.

MiniLM body128 substantially improves reach but increases fresh wrong grouped
pairs from 4 to 8 and mixed pages 7 to 10; it remains offline. Joint-title relevance
heads turn a few bad links into hundreds of wrong grouped relationships and
are rejected. Downloads went only to named TEMP directories; no remote code,
provider inference, corpus upload or additional extension model was used.

Indexed diagonal v2 and revision-bound cache passed independent Trust and
synthetic/actual-Chrome view checks. It counts identical coordinates once as
evidence, preserves every Source ID, avoids dense global pair allocation, and
supports 5,000-source synthetic cases. Ten-second/100-million-work guards,
synchronous blocking, bounded responses and dashboard rendering still remain.
It is not a claimed quality win or a no-limit production matcher.

The frozen dual-floor grid failed: its seven safe candidates all have zero
real-development grouped reach. No threshold rescue on fresh data follows.
The guarded coarse-indexed/negative-qualification/dual-complete-link module is
testable preparation only, not an activated matching policy.

The train-only linear transfer measured three regularization values and stopped
without a map meeting its frozen all-cohort coverage-nonregression screen. The
strongest fixed map, ridge-1, improves real-news grouped pairs 61 to 226 with
zero wrong joins, and C108 wrong pairs 58 to 20 while correct pairs rise 76 to
82. Its v6 correct coverage falls 79 to 53, so the original screen remains
failed. All these quality sets are reused development, not independent proof.
Independent review checked matrix orientation, label-free fitting and receipt
hashes. An overflowing-norm helper defect is blocked by this run's upstream
normalizer; preserve frozen code/results and guard future callers explicitly.

The owner's product priority is avoiding misassignments, not finding every
possible match on every slice. The lead therefore defines a **separate**
practical follow-up around fixed ridge-1; this is not a retroactive pass or
threshold rescue. Hold its fit, coefficients and calibration floor fixed,
compare the exact qualified indexed/coarse + complete-link refinement against
the same raw/diagonal composition on untouched whole events and independent v7.
Require greater overall correct/pure reach without greater wrong/mixed exposure
on either fresh real or synthetic set. No fresh-led parameter choice or retry.
Failure stops promotion. No client model, second retained vector, article
upload, canonical migration or automatic activation follows. Do not retune on
the exposed 296 cohort or imply the same-corpus holdout is publisher/viewpoint gold.

This materially improves overall reach and overall wrong exposure: 494/11

screen after seeing fresh results nor claiming there is no improvement is
justified. Grouped-pair precision and absolute wrong exposure are different
product trade-offs; these results warrant an explicit future decision, not
automatic promotion under the failed protocol.

All six plans are order-stable and complete without capacity failure; Ridge1
news planning took367.8ms. There is no catalog shadow, installer, retained
Ridge1 artifact or activation after failure. The running owner backend and New

fixed calibrated floors, not the deployed diagonal0.94/0.90 gates, so do not
