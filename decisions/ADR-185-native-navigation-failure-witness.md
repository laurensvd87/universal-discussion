## Distinguish the rejected blank loader without changing navigation authority

ADR184 preparation and its independent saved-output audit passed. Its actual
TRAIN/CAL attempt then failed in the first browser capture, before a batch drain,
E5 or fit. The closed diagnostic records `public-journey: blank-loader` during
article navigation. `Fetch.continueRequest` is the last noted RPC, not proof of
the failing operation. Cleanup passed without emergency termination; saved
source endchecks did not complete. All consumed v1/v2/v3/v4 source, maps, freezes,
outputs and profiles remain immutable.

The shared journey rejects blank-loader equality at navigation reply, commit,
admitted response, and post-begin lifecycle. The retained diagnostic does not
identify which path fired. The collector installs lifecycle callbacks before
dispatch; an exact reserved-blank replay arriving after begin is a possible
source-level race, not a proved historical cause. Do not move begin after the
navigation reply, remove listeners, sleep to hide timing or admit old-loader
evidence. No acceptance predicate changes in this successor.
