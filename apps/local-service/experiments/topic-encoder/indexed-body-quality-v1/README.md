# Indexed BODY policy comparison

Offline, aggregate-only owner-local research. The previously measured shared296
cohort (296 reports, 24 whole events) is reused to test the new indexed supported-
merge policy, not to claim a fresh independent holdout or tune a cutoff.

`node run.js --freeze` emits a descriptor/hash before any embedding or measuring.
After review, `node run.js --measure --freeze-hash <hash>` requires the exact same
sources, cohort selection, installed artifacts and policies. The same three
planners also measure reused authored C108 (18 events) and spent-v6 60 (12 events)
using their pinned project-created fixtures. All inputs are embedded with packaged
body-prefix E5; no fitting, downloads, provider, SQLite or
artifact installation occurs. Corpus text, labels, vectors and partitions remain
RAM-only. Output contains counts, artifact/source hashes and generic failures.

Raw E5 and the old diagonal use indexed independent-evidence v2 admission; BODY
uses the exact factory admission. Thus representation and admission both differ.
Pure/mixed multi-page exposure and correct/false grouped pairs evaluate whole
partitions. Viewpoint counters check authored opposing stances, and same-family
different-event counters are a nongold adjacent-story proxy. Neither establishes
real independent-publisher quality. Pair changes compare policy outputs only. Capacity failure yields no
partition or partial aggregate. This is a corpus-body surrogate, not real Chrome
capture, independent publisher/viewpoint evidence, legal clearance or a release.
