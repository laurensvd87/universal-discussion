# Chunk A — original multilingual training corpus

Training material only; this is not an independent holdout. Every place, institution and person below is fictional. Articles are authored from these fact sheets, not translated sentence by sentence. Each event has six distinct report voices; language coverage is en, nl, de, fr and es, with one language represented twice per event. The repeated language rotates en, nl, de, fr, es, en across the six entity families.

## Entity A01 — Lumaire ferry cooperative

### A01-E1 — North Quay night crossing begins
On 8 March 2025 the Lumaire Ferry Cooperative opened a night passenger crossing between North Quay and Bellstrand after a six-week trial. The battery vessel Lantern carries 48 people; the service runs hourly until 01:00. The outcome is a permanent timetable, funded by ticket sales and a town mobility grant.

### A01-E2 — West inlet stop added
On 19 April 2025 the cooperative added a floating stop at Reedmarket on the West Inlet route, effective the following Monday. Lantern remains on the North Quay crossing; a smaller boat, Brindle, serves Reedmarket three times each weekday. Market traders had requested the new stop.

### A01-E3 — Storm suspension and return
On 2 May 2025 high winds damaged the Bellstrand landing ramp and suspended the North Quay night crossing for four evenings. No passengers were hurt and Lantern was not damaged. A temporary shore gangway reopened the route on 7 May, while a permanent ramp repair was scheduled for June.

## Entity A02 — Veyra district seed library

### A02-E1 — Seed library opens
On 12 February 2025 the Veyra Seed Library opened inside the fictional Marrow Hall with eighty locally adapted vegetable varieties. Residents may borrow up to five labelled packets and are encouraged, but not required, to return saved seeds after harvest. The opening was organized by librarian Sela Orin and the district gardening circle; it creates a lending collection.

### A02-E2 — Heat tolerant collection announced
On 6 June 2025 the Veyra library added 24 heat-tolerant bean and squash varieties collected from growers in the fictional Sarn basin. The packets are available through the existing borrowing desk, with a short grower note attached. The aim is to give gardeners options during hotter summers.

### A02-E3 — Germination clinic postponed
On 14 September 2025 a burst pipe flooded the workshop room at Marrow Hall, forcing the Seed Library to postpone its free germination clinic from 16 to 30 September. The seed collection and lending desk remained open in the foyer. Staff moved the clinic to the dry reading room; no packets or records were lost. This is a schedule change after building damage.

## Entity A03 — Orbelin hill observatory

### A03-E1 — Public telescope night starts
On 21 January 2025 Orbelin Hill Observatory began a monthly public telescope night at the fictional Cinder Ridge site. Visitors can reserve one of 30 places and view the Moon and bright planets with volunteer guides. The first session is set for 24 January, weather permitting. The observatory created the event after completing a safety review of its eastern platform.

### A03-E2 — Cloud camera archive released
On 10 March 2025 the observatory released a six-month archive of cloud-camera images from its western dome, with timestamps and basic weather notes. The images are intended for classroom use and local sky watchers; astronomers cautioned that they are not calibrated scientific measurements. The archive offers a public record of local sky conditions.

### A03-E3 — Dome motor repaired
On 27 July 2025 a failed drive belt stopped the Orbelin observatory's western dome from rotating during a scheduled maintenance check. Technicians fitted a replacement part the next morning and resumed observations that evening. The eastern telescope and public program were unaffected. The observatory said the repair cost stayed within its routine maintenance budget; this is an equipment fix.

## Entity A04 — Caldrin community clinic

### A04-E1 — Evening nurse desk opens
On 3 April 2025 Caldrin Community Clinic opened a nurse-led evening desk on Tuesdays and Thursdays until 20:00. The walk-in service handles wound checks, medication questions and referrals, while urgent emergencies still go to the regional hospital. Director Iven Pell said the two-month pilot uses existing staff hours and will be reviewed in June. It is a new access window.

### A04-E2 — Mobile blood pressure visits
On 18 May 2025 the Caldrin clinic began monthly blood-pressure visits at three outlying villages, using a van shared with the district library service. Nurses will record readings only with each visitor's consent and refer concerning results to a clinician. The first stops are Deller, Moss End and Kestrel Fold. The program extends prevention visits beyond the clinic building.

### A04-E3 — Vaccine refrigerator alarm
On 9 August 2025 an overnight alarm showed that a refrigerator at Caldrin Clinic had exceeded its safe temperature range. Staff quarantined 36 vaccine doses and arranged replacement stock; no affected dose was administered. The clinic kept appointments open and contacted people whose visits might need rescheduling. An independent technician found a faulty sensor, which was replaced that afternoon.

## Entity A05 — Namar textile repair guild

### A05-E1 — Repair room opens
On 15 February 2025 the Namar Textile Guild opened a weekly repair room in the old fictional Tern weighhouse. Volunteers help residents mend clothing and teach basic stitching; visitors bring their own garments and materials. The guild received donated tables and a small municipal heating grant. Organizers describe the room as a practical skill-share.

### A05-E2 — Uniform mending agreement
On 11 May 2025 the guild signed a one-season agreement to mend school uniforms for Namar's three public schools. Families can request repairs through each school's office, and the guild will invoice the schools for thread and replacement fasteners only. The arrangement runs through December and adds scheduled work to the existing volunteer repair room.

### A05-E3 — Dye workshop canceled
On 22 October 2025 the guild canceled its outdoor indigo-dye workshop after runoff tests near the Tern weighhouse found residue from an unrelated drainage repair. The guild moved its indoor sewing session ahead by an hour and offered refunds for the dye class. Water tests later cleared the site, but organizers chose to wait for a covered wash station before rescheduling.

## Entity A06 — Pelloran marsh bird count

### A06-E1 — Winter count records increase
On 7 December 2024 volunteers in Pelloran's annual marsh count recorded 312 wintering birds across four marked sectors, 46 more than the previous year's tally. The largest increases were among grey waders and reed finches. Coordinator Mara Venn cautioned that weather and volunteer coverage affect counts; the figures are observations, not proof that the marsh population itself grew.

### A06-E2 — Boardwalk viewing hours extended
On 16 March 2025 the Pelloran marsh trust extended boardwalk access to 19:30 on Fridays and Saturdays through the spring migration period. A new volunteer rota will staff the south gate, where visitors must keep to the path and leave nesting reeds undisturbed. The decision followed requests from photographers and evening walkers.

### A06-E3 — Nesting zone temporarily closed
On 4 April 2025 marsh wardens closed the eastern spur of Pelloran's boardwalk after locating a heron nest unusually close to the path. The main loop and south hide remain open, and signs direct visitors around the 120-metre exclusion area. The trust plans a review on 20 May.

## Records and validation

`records.jsonl` contains exactly 108 rows: six families, three events per family and six reports per event. The JSON keys and order are fixed as `id,family,eventKey,lang,viewpoint,title,body`. The test checks exact structure, unique IDs, complete event/language coverage, title and body lengths, and the SHA-256 digest of the exact JSONL bytes. The digest is recorded in `integrity.test.js` and must be updated only when the authored corpus intentionally changes.
