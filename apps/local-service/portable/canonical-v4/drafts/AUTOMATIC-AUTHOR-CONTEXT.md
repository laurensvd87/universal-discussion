A draft binds Source material digest, stable subject ID and exact caption, not
evidence revision, Source write version, operation ID or current preferred leaf.
Thus identical-material renewals, additional supporting pages and a more specific
preferred subject do not retarget it. A page draft may finish with its original
page caption after a shared subject appears. Real material changes, erased
membership or a forged caption conflict and roll back all new canonical rows,
receipts and revisions. The UI must retain such a draft for review.

Exact replay checks both the original canonical command receipt and a separate
scope digest. An old schema17 command cannot be retrospectively relabelled by
replaying it through this port. A replay returns its historical revision even
after unrelated writes, and still succeeds after Source withdrawal.

reads expose neither Source material digest nor private prototype. Source forget
can erase every derived subject head/member/vector while existing author-owned
caption, human body and canonical destination remain. Withdrawing or hiding that
human root removes its authored caption. The separate receipt digest is used
only to compare an exact command retry, never to recover a discovered identity.
It is not indexed for matching or topic search.
