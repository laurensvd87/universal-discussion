This is the first technical milestone for the
[hierarchical assignment implementation](HIERARCHICAL_TOPIC_ASSIGNMENT_PLAN.md).
Resolve this matching problem before building the production hierarchy migration:
can the existing E5 page vector distinguish Games from GTA, or Nature from Koalas,
at useful coverage and low false-assignment rates?

The proposed solution keeps E5 fixed, represents each topic with several examples,
and evaluates a small classifier that can choose a child, stop at a parent, or
reject the branch. It does not expect one cosine threshold, a smaller vector, or
a cluster tree to establish semantic parenthood.

## What the existing vector contains

The packaged encoder returns one normalized 384-dimensional vector. Browser and
crawler both encode the bounded body text with `query: `, up to 4,096 UTF-16 code
units and 512 total tokens. Those 512 positions include two special tokens; the
remaining 510 token IDs include the query prefix, not 510 body tokens. The separate
page title is not concatenated into this embedding.

Shared encoding does not mean identical captured content: browser DOM extraction
and the crawler's static-HTML extraction have different fallbacks. Keep extractor
provenance in the evaluation and calibration slices. The current discussion
hierarchy uses the root's originating Source vector, not an embedding of its
question or replies; this plan must not imply that page vectors understand a
thread that has drifted to another subject.

The pinned Ridge adapter maps 384 dimensions to 384 dimensions and normalizes the
result. It is a learned change of geometry, not dimensionality reduction and not
a hierarchy classifier. Reusing it must not import its historical thresholds as
new broad-topic confidence rules.

Use the current contract as-is in the first experiment. The
[E5 model card](https://huggingface.co/intfloat/multilingual-e5-small/raw/main/README.md)
recommends the query prefix for classification features and symmetric matching;
it also explains why cosine scores are compressed toward high values. A score
such as 0.9 is not a 90 percent probability that the page belongs to GTA.

An important failure limit is missing input information. If the captured prefix
contains only navigation or discusses nature generally while the koala content is
outside it, no reduction or classifier can recover that absent evidence reliably.
Report these as input failures separately from matching failures. A later
title-plus-body or multi-section representation requires its own extraction,
privacy, browser/crawler parity, model identity and recapture/migration approval;
it is not a silent repair to existing vectors.

## Separate taxonomy from geometry

Maintain an explicit approved hierarchy with stable node IDs. Embeddings score
membership in those nodes; they do not decide whether Koalas is a subtopic of
Animals or merely related to Australia. Parent labels and edges need reviewed
definitions and provenance. Proposed new branches can be suggested offline,
but proximity alone does not activate or rename them.

Use the same page vector at every depth initially. The question changes by node:

| Decision | Positive evidence | Necessary counterexamples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Games membership | Diverse video games, board games and other approved descendants | Nature, finance, ordinary cars and unrelated acronym uses |
| Video games membership | GTA, other game franchises and general gaming pages | Board games and unrelated entertainment |
| GTA membership | Gameplay, reviews, modding and other in-scope GTA pages | Other franchises, generic gaming and non-game meanings of GTA |
| Koalas membership | Koala biology, conservation and other in-scope koala pages | Other animals and general nature pages that merely mention koalas |

A broad topic is a union of semantic regions. Averages over diverse descendants
can sit far from valid members, so a broad centroid is not a mandatory gate to
its children. Likewise, a narrow topic needs discriminating evidence relative to
siblings and generic-parent pages, not just a slightly higher global threshold.

## Build compact topic evidence

For each approved node, keep a bounded set of diverse positive exemplars and
confusing negative exemplars. Start by testing 2, 4 and 8 representative examples per
semantic mode, with an explicit total node/branch memory bound. These are experiment
settings, not a rule that a rare topic needs eight pages before it can exist.

Choose representatives from reviewed Source embeddings. Deduplicate syndicated
pages and limit the influence of one origin. Select actual examples near useful
modes rather than replacing all examples with one mean vector. Broad nodes reuse
diverse descendant evidence as well as genuinely broad pages; child evidence
does not require new copies of the original page text or vectors.

Every topic needs examples for these outcomes:

- Child subtree: the page is sufficiently about that child, including its descendants.
- Stop at parent: the page belongs to the parent, but is generic, mixed, or about
  an as-yet unrepresented child. A page about a new game must not be forced into GTA.
- Outside branch: the page does not belong to this parent. This is not the same
  outcome as a valid gaming page that cannot be assigned to a known franchise.

Owner-reviewed seed topics can begin with sparse evidence, but sparse evidence
does not earn an automatic high-confidence assignment. Broader fallback or
page-only handling remains available until the decision rule is validated.

## Experiment A Match using several examples

Establish a deterministic classifier without training new encoder weights.
For each candidate topic, compute a small feature packet from the normalized
page vector and its reviewed exemplars:

- Best positive match and the mean of the strongest independent positive matches.
- Number and diversity of supporting examples, with fixed normalization for
  evidence-set size so broad topics do not win simply by having more examples.
- Strongest confusing-negative match and the positive-minus-negative margin.
- Margin over the strongest sibling, plus evidence for generic-parent stopping.
- Distance from known positive regions, as a warning for out-of-scope inputs.

Choose feature combinations and acceptance rules on development data, not by
inventing production constants in advance. Compare pinned Ridge features with
unchanged raw E5 features in separate shadow runs; the latter is a challenger,
never an automatic fallback or a change to existing canonical matching.

Initially retrieve exactly across all topic exemplar sets within a declared
prototype-count bound, then expand ancestors. This permits a strong GTA example
to nominate Games even if a Games centroid would miss it. Compare results with
an exhaustive reference implementation. Stop at the deepest supported node;
accepting a child inherits its approved ancestry without a second unrelated
parent-centroid requirement.

Evidence counts and sibling margins are signals, not sufficient proof by
themselves. One child winning every comparison still does not establish that the
page belongs anywhere in that branch. Unknown topics and generic-parent examples
must remain in the validation set.

## Experiment B Learn small decision heads on frozen E5

If the exemplar baseline leaves useful information in the 384-dimensional vector
unused, train regularized linear membership heads on the same fixed vectors.
Each head is a dot product and bias, not a second transformer or an LLM call.
Training occurs offline; runtime performs only bounded matrix/vector arithmetic.

For a topic node t, the raw membership score is `a_t · z + b_t`, where z is the
selected frozen representation. Broad heads train on the union of diverse
descendant positives and broad pages, not only paragraphs containing the parent
name. Local child heads train against siblings, stop-at-parent pages and outside-
branch negatives. Include data with missing/unrepresented child topics. A single
linear broad head may underfit a union of separated semantic regions; it remains
a challenger to multi-exemplar matching, not its presumed replacement.

Prefer independent membership heads where genuine multi-branch membership is
allowed. If a local sibling classifier uses softmax, include explicit stop and
outside outcomes; a softmax over known children alone would always select one.
Do not renormalize selected candidates and interpret that as confidence that
the correct topic was retrieved. Measure whole-path decisions after calibration,
not just isolated head accuracy.

Fit score calibration and stopping rules on a separate calibration split. Each
node or supported depth uses a measured acceptance rule; scarce-data nodes use
a tested pooled rule or abstain. Never multiply child probabilities and call the
result guaranteed path confidence. Compare precision and useful coverage with
Experiment A before choosing the extra training/storage complexity.

Only if A and B fail a documented error analysis should Experiment C test a
learned broad/fine projection or another input representation. Any fitted
projection uses training data only and receives a new representation identity.
PCA/UMAP/HDBSCAN are optional offline challengers, not required production stages.
Reducing 384 dimensions to 10 is not intrinsically a broad-to-specific classifier.

## Choose the most specific justified result

The decision procedure must distinguish weak child evidence from an invalid parent:

1. Gather candidate nodes from diverse exemplar matches or the evaluated frozen
   heads. Keep more than one viable branch; do not make an irreversible top-one
   choice at the root. Include explicit ancestor expansion and outside cases.
2. Score node membership, sibling separation, known-region support and stopping
   outcomes using the selected calibrated artifact.
3. Accept a child only if its full acceptance rule passes. A strong accepted leaf
   supplies ancestry through approved edges; it does not need every ancestor to
   exceed the same cosine number.
4. If siblings remain ambiguous, return their supported ancestor. Two weak
   sibling matches alone do not prove that ancestor membership is valid.
5. If the page belongs to a parent but no child qualifies, stop at that parent.
   If no branch qualifies, keep the page unresolved/page-only. Preserve multiple
   paths only when each independently passes the multi-topic rule.
6. Return the chosen path, primary assignment, decision reason and exact Source,
   evidence, taxonomy, representation and calibration versions.

The output can be Games → Video games without a franchise, or Nature → Animals
without a species. It must never invent a specific assignment just to fill a
predefined number of hierarchy levels. Owner display depth does not override
these acceptance rules.

Evaluate abstention as a precision/coverage trade-off. The
[selective classification literature](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/hash/4a8423d5e91fda00bb7e46540e2b0cf1-Abstract.html)
provides the reject-option principle; its published image-classification results
do not establish guarantees for this project's pages or taxonomy.

## Data and evaluation before integration

Create new reviewed examples with gold primary paths, permitted secondary paths,
and deepest justified depth. Include broad-only pages, unknown children, unknown
branches, comparisons, incidental mentions, rare topics and multilingual inputs.
Include misleading titles and body-prefix failures to separate input quality from
vector geometry. Games/GTA and Nature/Koalas are required cases, not the whole set.

Keep training, development, calibration and locked final test sets separate.
Split duplicates, translations, publishers and closely related article series as
groups to prevent leakage; include later-time and unseen-site tests. Include a
withheld-child test: hide a known game from training and require Video games or
unresolved rather than a wrong familiar leaf. Old frozen cohorts and the earlier
84-text experiment are not new tuning data.

Measure these components independently:

- Candidate recall for the gold accepted paths before final classification.
- Precision and coverage at each depth and per supported language/branch.
- Wrong top-level branches, over-specific assignments and needless broad stopping.
- Unknown-topic rejection, sibling ambiguity and validated secondary assignments.
- Stability under equivalent captures and taxonomy/example updates.
- End-to-end errors and latency, including routing failures rather than only
  accuracy conditional on having retrieved the correct candidate.

Carry forward the implementation plan's proposed 98 percent specific-assignment
precision and at most 1 percent wrong broad branches, with sufficient independent
test support and uncertainty reporting. These are release targets, not measured
results or fixed cosine values. Reject a system that achieves precision by
abstaining on practically everything; select a minimum useful coverage target
from the first labelled-data baseline before locking the final test.

## Performance and update design

One existing E5 vector serves every depth. Transform it at most once per selected
representation, and reuse its validated exact-input decision on unchanged visits.
Example retrieval, optional linear heads and stopping rules run over the compact
taxonomy artifacts, not every crawled page and not a whole-catalog cluster fit.

Start with an exact bounded reference implementation. Introduce beam routing or
an approximate prototype index only after measuring missed accepted leaves versus
that reference. Report finite node/prototype counts, fan-out, memory and time
limits. Capacity exhaustion returns an explicit status; it must not masquerade as
an evidence-based broad assignment.

Benchmark classification after the embedding exists separately from encoding,
queue wait, PostgreSQL commit, catalog validation and first-feed loading. Target
p95 at most 200ms for the bounded classifier, under concurrent crawler work; do
not turn microsecond dot products into a claim of 200ms whole-page readiness.

New approved examples and corrections update artifacts in the background following
their actual dependency graph. That can include ancestors, sibling hard negatives,
other branches and pooled calibration, not only the edited subtree. Version them
atomically; changed inputs or evidence invalidate old decisions. Do not self-train
on automatic accepted labels without review,
count duplicate crawler pages as independent evidence, or retain erased examples
inside old prototypes. Corrections first improve the evidence and labelled set;
they do not silently weaken acceptance thresholds. Forgetting or revoking an example
must also invalidate and rebuild any dependent learned heads/calibrators and handle
retained training artifacts under the agreed deletion policy. Removing an exemplar
row alone does not remove its contribution to fitted weights; affected decisions
must not remain eligible while repair runs.

## Deliverables and the implementation gate

1. Freeze the input audit, new labelled taxonomy cases and evaluation protocol.
2. Implement the exact multi-exemplar baseline, with raw and pinned-Ridge shadow
   comparisons and explicit parent-stop/outside behaviour.
3. Train and compare small frozen-feature heads only where justified by errors;
   calibrate independently and lock the selected artifact before final testing.
4. Produce a decision report containing quality/coverage curves, per-depth errors,
   missing-input cases, resource limits, version hashes and exact reproducibility.
5. Proceed to hierarchy storage, shadow backfill and reviewed runtime activation
   only if the selected method passes those gates. If it does not, record whether
   the blocker is insufficient labels, inadequate captured input or inadequate
   vector separability; do not compensate with untested broad merging.
