# Preliminary trademark screening for Murmingle

Date: 12 October 2026. Requested markets: European Union, United Kingdom and
United States. Live register checks ran between 10:52 and 11:12 CEST
(08:52–09:12 UTC).

No exact **Murmingle** record appeared in the completed EUIPO, USPTO, WIPO or
TMview searches. This supports retaining the name for further assessment, but
does not establish legal clearance. Relevant registered **MURMUR** and
**MINGLE** component marks cover software, forums or social networking. The
UKIPO direct search and registered-logo similarity search remain incomplete.

This is a preliminary factual screen, not a legal opinion, registration,
permission to launch, or assurance against infringement. No app behavior,
brand artwork, provider flow or running service was changed by this research.

## Product and search scope

The proposed mark identifies Murmingle's downloadable browser extension and
planned services bringing discussions about the same subject together across
pages. The approved logo is **A — Shared space**, two opposing speech shapes
with their overlap left open. It is not the earlier M-in-a-bubble placeholder.
See [the brand guide](../docs/BRAND_LANGUAGE.md).

The main goods and services examined were downloadable software in class 9,
forums/chat and communications in class 38, and SaaS/software hosting in class
42. Social-network services in class 45 also matter, as the US candidate below
demonstrates. These are search categories, not a finalized filing specification.
Exact searches did not exclude marks through class or active-status filters.
Similarity and related goods can cross class boundaries.
[WIPO class 38](https://nclpub.wipo.int/enfr/?class_number=38&explanatory_notes=show&lang=en&menulang=en),

## Marketplace and logo checks

The bounded web searches did not verify an operating software/forum business
using the exact name Murmingle. **MurMingle** appears in a poster-company
[name-ideas list](https://theideavault.blog/275-catchy-poster-company-name-ideas-to-inspire-your-brand/);
that is not evidence of actual trade or registered rights.

Primary commercial sites demonstrate nearby naming in related fields:
[Infor Ming.le](https://www.infor.com/products/infor-ming-le) is enterprise
social collaboration software; [Murmur Connect](https://murmurconnect.com/)
provides messaging between different people's AI assistants; the
[Mumble project](https://www.mumble.info/downloads/) also calls its voice-chat
server Murmur. These uses do not prove a conflict with the complete Murmingle
name, but matter to a broader marketplace search.

General image searches found existing overlapping speech-bubble designs,
including [Bagas's Letter S exploration](https://dribbble.com/shots/26164361-Letter-S-Logo-Design-Exploration).
Its image was inspected; it uses outlined interlocking speech forms, not an
established exact copy of the approved filled Shared space logo. Sharing a
general motif does not establish infringement, and this web check cannot clear
the logo against registered figurative marks.

The attempted TMview image-search upload of the approved 128px PNG was rejected
by automatic approval review because explicit authorization to send the local
logo to that third-party service was missing. **No upload occurred.** Registry
image similarity searching remains pending approval; no search result is
inferred from that blocked action. No upload through an alternate route was used.
