Source-only, default inert, not prepared or authorized to run. The closed ADR168
attempt is preserved unchanged; its exact failure cause remains unknown. This
successor tests an explicit navigation rendezvous, not public-page access,
semantic matching quality, native write authority or an OS egress sandbox.

The private journey joins five actual facts on the existing article CDP session:
the settled `Page.navigate` reply, main-frame commit, Document response,
loader-bound `DOMContentLoaded`, and successful physical settlement of the one
`route.fulfill` promise. Every fact must agree on the independently authenticated
frame, fresh loader and fixture URL wherever that protocol supplies them. No
arrival chooses the loader by itself. All120 arrival orders are supported.

The initial blank frame/loader is captured independently. Only exact blank
lifecycle events before dispatch may be ignored. Other non-DCL lifecycle names
come from one literal enum, remain counted, and quarantine one loader candidate
which must agree with every subsequent fact. Unknown, stale, subframe,
contradictory, duplicate and retired callbacks fail closed, including after the
five-fact promise resolves. Reports expose only candidate-presence/agreement,
never its raw loader. The unchanged owner commit still executes at the real
callback; its original failure takes precedence over additional observation.
Unchanged `verifyNavigation` executes once after all five facts. A transport
drain that swallowed a rejection cannot synthesize fulfillment success.
